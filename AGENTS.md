## Development

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.

## AI article production

When asked to create, write, add, or research a HOBNOVA article or content item—even when the
request is only 「HOBNOVAの記事を1本作って。テーマも任せる」—read and follow
[`docs/ai-article-guidelines.md`](docs/ai-article-guidelines.md) before making changes. It is the
required end-to-end workflow and completion gate for topic selection, research, writing, images,
implementation, review, validation, and the human-approved publishing flow. Do not treat an
article as complete unless every item in that guide's completion checklist passes.
